# Bankside

A fishing log for UK anglers that runs on your phone. Catches, photos, personal bests, venues, a species checklist and automatic weather.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free, about 10 minutes, on a computer)

1. Sign in at github.com and click **New repository**. Name it `bankside`, choose **Public**, tick **Add a README file**, and click **Create repository**.
2. Unzip `bankside-app.zip` on your computer.
3. In the new repository, click **Add file** then **Upload files**. Drag in everything from the unzipped folder (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the `fish` and `icons` folders). Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings**, then **Pages**. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
5. After a minute or two, the page shows your link, like `https://yourname.github.io/bankside/`.

## Install it on your Android phone

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** at the top of Bankside, or Chrome's menu (three dots) then **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open Bankside from the new icon. Allow location when it asks, so GPS and weather work.
4. Go to **Records**, then **Restore from a backup**, and choose `bankside-start.json` to bring over your species ticks.

## Looking after your data

Everything is stored on your phone. Use **Records → Back up now** regularly and keep the file in Google Drive or email. A new phone gets your log back with **Restore from a backup**.
