# Waggler

A fishing log for UK anglers that runs on your phone. Catches, photos, personal bests, venues, a species checklist with ID notes, and automatic weather.

## Install it on your Android phone

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** at the top of Waggler, or Chrome's menu (three dots) then **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open Waggler from the new icon. Allow location when it asks, so GPS and weather work.

## Looking after your data

Everything is stored on your phone. Use **More → Back up now** regularly and keep the file in Google Drive or email. A new phone gets your log back with **Restore from a backup**. Backups made under the old name, Bankside, still restore.
